Methodology

Transparent by default

The displayed field is a screening-grade, two-dimensional energy-balance scenario. It combines real building footprints and cited intervention assumptions, but it is not a measured ward raster or an engineering specification.

  • Model response is relative to the selected baseline.
  • Capital estimates are indicative and should be presented as ranges.
  • Measured surface-temperature data remains the publication gate.
