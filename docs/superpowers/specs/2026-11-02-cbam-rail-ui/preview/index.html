4 · What this does not tell you

  • The cross-sectoral correction factor (CSCF) for 2026–2030 is unpublished. Each figure above is therefore a floor: the real bill cannot be lower than what is shown, and may be higher once the true factor is published.
  • Article 9 deductions for a carbon price paid in the country of origin are not modelled (the implementing act is still a draft), so figures do not credit any such payment.
  • Any below-threshold verdict rests on the user's own statement of completeness, ticked in the tool. No one has verified that list.

Framing · read first

Prototype estimator · Commission default values or your own verified figures · decision-support, not a declaration. Computed in your browser from the published rules, and never sent anywhere. This tool counts certificates for 2026, 2027 and 2028 imports, and shows a cost only where the Commission has published a certificate price. For 2026 the price is set per quarter. From 2027 it is set weekly: none published as at 14 October 2026, so a 2027 or 2028 import shows certificates and no cost. No final figure exists for any of these years — the cross-sectoral correction factor is unpublished — so any number below is a labelled what-if. This is not a filing, not validated by the EU CBAM Registry, and not a statement of monetary liability.

Not yet published

  • Cross-sectoral correction factor (CSCF)

    Certificate counts assume no cross-sectoral correction: the Commission has not published the factor for these years, and it can only reduce the free allocation deducted.

  • 2027 and later: weekly certificate prices
