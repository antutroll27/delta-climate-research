# Funding landscape

Grants, credits, accelerators, climate/adaptation VC, dev-finance, and commercial wedges — plus the
verified dead-ends and the raise strategy. This is the money map behind the credibility-moat thesis.

> Compiled from `docs/research/2026-08-09-climate-funding-landscape.md` (primary source, ~12 research
> passes, 2026-08-09) cross-checked against the co-founder briefing's Sources §09–10
> (`docs/briefings/2026-08-09-twin-credibility-briefing.html`, [15]–[27]). Cheque sizes and eligibility
> are as-found on primary/official pages; **application windows rotate — reconfirm before applying.**

**The strategy in one line:** lead with the credibility moat; use non-dilutive money + a climate
accelerator to de-risk and stamp credibility, then raise a climate-VC seed. Grants fund the proof, the
proof wins the seed, the seed funds the scale. Rationale: adaptation *software* is the funded lane (~71%
of adaptation capital goes to software/data; resilience was the fastest-growing climate-tech category in
H1 2025) [15], and the #1 diligence risk is "is this real?" — 74% of climate-AI claims are unproven [16].
Free EO data is not a moat; calibration, ground-truth, and published validation are.

  openly admit the basis risk (a 3–3.5°C ERA5-vs-IMD gap; 2023 = hottest year, *zero* payout). A

## Strategy notes

- **The unlock:** DPIIT Startup Recognition (free) → an incubator that is simultaneously SISFS + DST-NIDHI
  TBI + AIC → one admission opens three non-dilutive tracks at once.
- **"Validation is the moat, not the data."** Free EO/satellite data is commodity; calibration,
  ground-truth, and published out-of-sample accuracy are what funders diligence against (74% of climate-AI
  claims are unproven — precisely the risk the twin's validation dossier answers). [16][25]
- **Parametric heat-insurance wedge:** SEWA / Swiss Re / ICICI Lombard already run parametric heat products
  in Gujarat on crude single-station/ERA5 triggers with admitted basis risk (documented 2023 zero-payout
  despite the hottest year) — the commercial adjacency where a validated high-resolution surface becomes a
  sellable data input, independent of the grant/VC track.
- **12–18 month sequence:** Months 0–3, all non-dilutive (DPIIT, Phase-1 receipts + validation dossier,
  NVIDIA Inception + cloud credits, a multi-accredited incubator). Months 3–9, prove it (SISFS + NIDHI +
  Climate Pitch, a global accelerator stamp, 1–2 municipal/academic pilots, Phase-2 receipts). Months
  9–18, raise the seed (Avaana / Transition / Ankur / Speciale + ADB Ventures) on the de-risked story.

**Flagged uncertainties:** Villgro's sector fit; Green Frontier/Theia/Omnivore detail;
ANRF/Belmont/Horizon Europe specifics; and the Munich Re Ventures wind-down date are all marked "(verify)"
or ⚠️ because the source docs list them without independent confirmation. ADB Ventures (~$200k, quasi-VC
equity) and ADB Challenges (≤$450k, grants) are kept distinct to avoid conflating cheque sizes.
