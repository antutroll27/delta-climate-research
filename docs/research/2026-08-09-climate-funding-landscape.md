## The strategy in one line
Lead with the **credibility moat**; use **non-dilutive money + a climate accelerator** to de-risk and
stamp credibility, **then** raise a **climate-VC seed**. Grants fund the proof · the proof wins the seed ·
the seed funds the scale.

**Why the moat matters to funders:** adaptation *software* is the funded lane (~71% of adaptation capital
goes to software/data; resilience was the fastest-growing climate-tech category in H1 2025), and the #1
diligence risk is "is this real?" — **74% of climate-AI claims are unproven**. Free EO data is *not* a
moat; our calibration, ground-truth and published validation are. That rigor is the single strongest
fundraising asset we have.

## 1 · Non-dilutive — India government (do these first)

**The unlock:** get **DPIIT Startup Recognition** (free, online — [startupindia.gov.in](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/)),
which enables SISFS + the 80-IAC tax holiday, then join **one incubator that is simultaneously a SISFS
incubator + a DST-NIDHI TBI + an Atal Incubation Centre** — that single admission opens SISFS, NIDHI and
AIC support at once.

| Program | Cheque | Equity? | Gotcha | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SISFS** (Startup India Seed Fund) | ₹20L grant (PoC) + up to ₹50L (market entry) | ₹20L = grant; ₹50L = convertible/debt | DPIIT-recognised, **incorporated ≤2 yrs**, ≥51% Indian; disbursed via a chosen incubator (not DPIIT); <₹10L prior central/state funding | [seedfund.startupindia.gov.in](https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/) |
| **DST NIDHI-SSS** | up to ₹1 cr | via incubator, often soft/convertible | must be an incubatee of a DST-supported TBI ≥3 months first; not for MNC subsidiaries | [nidhi.dst.gov.in](https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/) |
| **DST NIDHI-PRAYAS** | up to ₹10L | grant | via a PRAYAS centre; prototype/hardware-leaning culture | [nidhi.dst.gov.in](https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/schemes-programmes/nidhiprayas/) |
| **MeitY TIDE 2.0** | ₹4L (EiR) / ₹7L (prototype) | grant | ICT/software eligible (cleantech listed); via 51 incubators; mind the ₹10L SISFS cross-cap | [msh.meity.gov.in](https://msh.meity.gov.in/) |
| **MeitY GENESIS** | up to ₹50L (matching) | grant (matching) | **excludes Tier-I cities — Kolkata likely ineligible** | [msh.meity.gov.in](https://msh.meity.gov.in/) |
| **Atal Innovation Mission (AIC)** | none direct (to you) | — | AIM funds the incubator, not startups; you access SISFS/NIDHI *through* an AIC | [aim.gov.in](https://aim.gov.in/) |

**Impact-incubators that disburse the above + add mentoring/pilots:**
- **Climate Collective — Climate Pitch** — equity-free **₹20L grant**, explicit *Adaptation & Resilience /
  urban-resilience* theme, for-profit India required, prototype/pilot stage, rev ₹0–50L. Cleanest thematic
  fit found. [climatepitch.org](https://climatepitch.org/) · [climatecollective.net](https://climatecollective.net/)
- **Social Alpha — Techtonic: Sustainable Urban Transition** — pilot grant up to **₹35L** (+ up to ₹1cr
  equity); names *thermal comfort* in scope. [socialalpha.org](https://www.socialalpha.org/)
- **Villgro** — seed ~₹25–50L (grant/debt/equity mix); SISFS disburser; agri/health-leaning. [villgro.org](https://villgro.org/programs/)
- **DST NM-ICPS Technology Innovation Hubs** — per-hub grants ~₹10–50L; **AI4ICPS at IIT Kharagpur (in West Bengal)** is in-state. [indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/st-visions/national-mission/national-mission-interdisciplinary-cyber-physical-systems-nm-icps)
- **T-Hub AIC Sustainability** — no cheque, ₹50k+GST fee, **but strong govt/city/corporate pilot access**. [t-hub.co](https://t-hub.co/aic-sustainability-program)

**India heat-specific challenges:** **Greenovation Urban Climate Resilience Challenge** (EarthON) — ₹20L
non-dilutive + up to ₹1cr follow-on; "heat resilience" + "climate data tools" named themes
([earthonfoundation.org](https://earthonfoundation.org/greenovation-urban-climate-resilience-challenge/)) ·
**Keep Cool Innovation Challenge** (WRI India + Social Alpha) — heat, people-centred framing
([wri.org](https://www.wri.org/initiatives/keep-cool-innovation-challenge-cool-solutions-warming-world)).

## 2 · Non-dilutive runway — cloud + data credits (verified)

- **NVIDIA Inception** — equity-free, rolling; DGX + up to $100k AWS credits + VC intros. Apply day one. [nvidia.com/startups](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)
- **Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** — up to **$150k Azure, no equity**. [microsoft.com/startups](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups)
- **AWS Activate** — up to $100k (via partner/VC referral). [aws.amazon.com/startups](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)
- **Google for Startups Cloud** — up to $200k (std) / $350k (AI-first needs Vertex/Gemini + VC funding). [cloud.google.com/startup](https://cloud.google.com/startup)

**Free EO backbones (commercial-use OK) — the open-data pitch rests on these:**
[Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/) · [MS Planetary Computer](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/) · AWS Open Data (Landsat/Sentinel) · NASA LP DAAC (ECOSTRESS).
**Traps:** Google Earth Engine free tier is **non-commercial only** (a for-profit product needs a paid
commercial licence — [earthengine.google.com/commercial](https://earthengine.google.com/commercial/)); **Planet NICFI free imagery ended Jan 2025** (don't cite it).

## 4 · Climate / adaptation VC (the seed leg)

- **India, on-thesis:** **Avaana Capital** (most on-thesis India climate) · Transition VC · Ankur Capital ·
  Speciale Invest (deep-tech/space/geospatial at pre-seed) · Green Frontier / Theia / Omnivore.
  [avaana.capital](https://avaana.capital/)
- **Adaptation specialists:** **Lightsmith Group** — the marquee adaptation/resilience fund ($186M), names
  *geospatial intelligence + catastrophe risk*; **growth equity, not pre-seed** → "know early, raise later"
  ([lightsmithgp.com](https://lightsmithgp.com/)). **Space Capital / Seraphim Space** — geospatial/EO VCs, US/UK-centric, later.
- **Emerging-market / impact:** **Mercy Corps Ventures** (Global-South adaptation; tailored impact KPIs) ·
  **ADB Ventures** (seed grant ~$200k + equity; India-eligible; quasi-VC).
- **What they want at pre-seed/seed:** a named buyer + who-pays model; a wedge pilot/**priced LOI** (not a
  waitlist); **validated accuracy as the moat**; impact in beneficiary terms (people protected /
  heat-exposure reduced) *alongside* revenue. Segment funders: catalytic/grant first, commercial VC second.
  Sources: [tran.vc](https://www.tran.vc/how-to-use-lois-waitlists-and-pilots-in-pre-seed-fundraising/) ·
  [msci-institute.com](https://www.msci-institute.com/themes/climate/how-to-make-climate-adaptation-and-resilience-investable/) ·
  [UNEP FI / ARIC impact framework](https://www.unepfi.org/themes/climate-change/adaptation-resilience-impact-a-measurement-framework-for-investors/)

## 5 · Dev-finance, multilateral & philanthropy (mostly indirect / vendor)

- **ADB Challenges** — grants ≤ ~**$450k, startups eligible, India challenges run** — the best *direct*
  non-dilutive dev-finance door (watch for a heat/urban theme). [challenges.adb.org](https://challenges.adb.org/web/en/challenges)
- **Grand Challenges India** (BIRAC + Gates) — up to **$200k, Indian for-profits eligible**; **already
  funded a Karnataka heatwave-resilience project** — best philanthropy precedent (monitor next climate-health
  cycle). [gcgh.grandchallenges.org](https://gcgh.grandchallenges.org/challenge/grand-challenges-india-accelerating-catalyzing-solutions-climate-changes-impact-health)
- **GCF / Adaptation Fund** — **do not fund startups directly**; only via an Accredited Entity (in India,
  **NABARD**). Real path = be the analytics vendor/executing entity on an AE-led city project.
- **World Bank / GIZ / AFD (CITIIS 2.0, incl. New Town Kolkata) / UK PACT** — fund *cities/governments*;
  your route is procurement/subcontracting, not a grant. World Bank's July-2025 India cities report names
  **Kolkata, urban heat islands (+3–4°C), and a $2.4T resilience need** — the best demand-side citation.
  [worldbank.org](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/07/22/india-has-a-critical-opportunity-to-drive-resilient-urban-development-says-new-world-bank-report)
- **IDRC/FCDO CLARE** — Global-South adaptation (India eligible), but research-institute/NGO-led. [idrc-crdi.ca](https://idrc-crdi.ca/en/initiative/climate-adaptation-and-resilience-clare)

**Academic-grant route (startup = named industry partner, academic = grantee):**
- **Wellcome Climate Impacts Awards** — up to **£2.5M**, LMIC-open, *heat→health made visible* — best
  thematic fit; needs an India academic host (IIT-KGP / Jadavpur / IISc). [wellcome.org](https://wellcome.org/)
- **DST Urban Climate Research & Extreme Events / NMSKCC** — names urban-heat-island by name; Indian PI leads. [dst.gov.in](https://dst.gov.in/climate-change-programme)
- **ANRF** (ex-SERB) industry-academia grants; **Belmont Forum** climate-health CRAs; **Horizon Europe**
  (India = non-associated third country: can partner, must self-fund the Indian side).

## 6 · Adjacencies — buyers, not funders

- **Parametric heat-insurance (the commercial wedge, in our launch geography):** SEWA · Swiss Re · ICICI
  Lombard (Gujarat) and Mahila Housing Trust · Go Digit trigger on **crude single-station / ERA5 data** and
  **openly admit the basis risk** (a 3–3.5°C ERA5-vs-IMD gap; 2023 = hottest year, *zero* payout). Our
  validated, high-resolution, night-and-humidity-aware surface is the fix → we're a **data-input vendor to
  underwriters/reinsurers**. [inclusivecapitalism.com](https://www.inclusivecapitalism.com/news-insights/parametric-insurance-sewa/) ·
  [downtoearth.org.in](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/climate-change/can-parametric-insurance-really-put-a-price-on-extreme-heat-and-protect-indias-informal-workers-from-its-growing-economic-toll)
  · Swiss Re Public Sector Solutions (data-buyer/structurer). ⚠️ **Munich Re Ventures is winding down (Q2 2026)** — not a pre-seed source.
- **B2G / heat-resilience buyers:** **no Indian city has a Chief Heat Officer yet** (help stand up the
  first = a champion) · **Chennai Resilience Center** (CRO at IIT-Madras) + **NIUA Climate Centre for
  Cities** = warmest doors · Kolkata is a **C40 member but absent from C40's Cool Cities Network** (a sharp
  pitch to KMC / WB SDMA) · NDMA/Heat Action Plans = sales channel, not grants · UNEP Cool Coalition =
  partnership/visibility. [onebillionresilient.org](https://heatactionplatform.onebillionresilient.org/) ·
  [resilientcitiesnetwork.org/chennai](https://resilientcitiesnetwork.org/chennai/) · [niua.in/c-cube](https://niua.in/c-cube) ·
  [c40.org cool-cities](https://www.c40.org/accelerators/cool-cities/)
- **Carbon: NOT a fit.** No credit methodology exists for cooling/heat-reduction; urban-tree registries
  credit sequestration only and are US-only. Position as adaptation, not carbon.
- **Strategic/exit horizon:** physical-climate-risk analytics gets acquired (Moody's→RMS $2B; S&P→The
  Climate Service). Investor narrative, not a near-term ask.

## 7 · The 12–18 month sequence
1. **Months 0–3 (all non-dilutive):** DPIIT recognition; ship Phase-1 receipts + publish the **validation
   dossier**; claim NVIDIA Inception + cloud credits; build on Copernicus/Planetary Computer; apply to a
   multi-accredited incubator.
2. **Months 3–9 (prove it):** SISFS + NIDHI + Climate Pitch; a global accelerator (Third Derivative /
   Katapult) for the stamp; land **1–2 municipal/academic pilots** (Chennai CRC / NIUA / a Kolkata HAP);
   Phase-2 receipts (measured-vs-modelled + validation) as the defensibility artifact; queue an IIT/IISc co-PI.
3. **Months 9–18 (raise the seed):** pitch Avaana / Transition / Ankur / Speciale + ADB Ventures on the
   de-risked story; convert pilots to paid B2G; open the insurance/reinsurance adjacency.

## 8 · Grant-application checklist (do-first order)
- [ ] Incorporate as an Indian Pvt Ltd (≥51% Indian; note SISFS's ≤2-yr-old cap — apply early).
- [ ] **DPIIT recognition** (free) → unlocks SISFS + 80-IAC tax + most schemes.
- [ ] Shortlist incubators that are **SISFS + DST-NIDHI TBI + AIC** simultaneously; apply to up to 3.
- [ ] Claim **NVIDIA Inception → Microsoft Founders Hub / AWS Activate** (runway, no equity).
- [ ] Publish the **validation dossier** (out-of-sample skill + provenance) — the artifact every later step leans on.
- [ ] File **SISFS**, **Climate Pitch**, **Social Alpha Techtonic**; watch **ADB Challenges** + **Grand Challenges India** windows.
- [ ] Land a **priced pilot/LOI** with the right municipal owner (disaster/health/planning) — not a waitlist.
- [ ] Keep the stack commercially clean (avoid Earth Engine commercial licence; use Copernicus/AWS Open Data).

## 9 · Do-not-chase (verified dead-ends)
USAID (defunct 2025) · BIRAC (biotech-only) · MeitY GENESIS (excludes Tier-I → Kolkata) · ESA / NASA / EU
direct grants (India-ineligible without an EU entity) · GCF & Adaptation Fund (only via an accredited
entity such as NABARD) · Google Earth Engine free tier for a commercial product · Planet NICFI (ended) ·
carbon credits for cooling (no methodology) · Munich Re Ventures (winding down).
