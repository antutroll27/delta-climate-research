# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Delta Climate Research serves institutional decision-makers, municipalities, corporate sustainability teams, and regional partners across India and the United Arab Emirates. Visitors need to assess the studio's credibility, understand its research methods, and decide whether to begin a serious climate-intelligence or adaptation engagement.

## Product Purpose

The site establishes Delta Climate Research as the interpretation layer between raw earth-science data and consequential decisions. It should make the studio's interdisciplinary capability legible, demonstrate standards-aligned rigor, publish research and project evidence, and convert qualified visitors into access or contact requests.

## Brand Personality

Instrument-grade, calm, and exacting. The experience should feel like a high-fidelity planetary instrument: technically ambitious, methodologically transparent, and confident without becoming theatrical or alarmist.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon crypto aesthetics, generic climate-consultancy greenwashing, anonymous SaaS card grids, shallow dashboard theater, and claims that are not supported by visible evidence. Motion and technical visuals must clarify the narrative rather than substitute for substance.

## Design Principles

1. Show the method behind every claim.
2. Put evidence before theory and conversion after credibility.
3. Preserve an instrument-like visual language without sacrificing plain-language comprehension.
4. Use ambitious motion and WebGL only when they remain performant, optional, and resilient.
5. Treat publication state, metadata, and source accuracy as part of the product experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All essential content and actions must work with keyboard and assistive technology, maintain AA contrast, remain usable at 200% text zoom, and provide static equivalents when reduced motion is requested or WebGL is unavailable. Mobile and lower-powered devices must receive complete content without depending on desktop choreography.
